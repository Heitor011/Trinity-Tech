## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a clean, high-precision technical presence tailored for professional hardware maintenance, diagnostic servicing, and preventive care for computers and notebooks. The visual ethos merges corporate reliability with modern digital precision—evoking surgical cleanliness, speed, and engineering competence.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Everyday professionals, remote workers, gamers, and small business owners who require prompt, dependable, transparent computer repairs and thermal/hardware servicing.
- **Emotional Response:** Absolute peace of mind, technical authority, and transparency. The interface communicates prompt turnaround times, hygienic handling, and certified hardware care.

### Aesthetic Direction
A tailored hybrid of **Corporate / Modern** and **High-Contrast Precision**:
- Crisp, clinical white backgrounds paired with deep navy structure and vibrant electric cyan accents derived directly from the logo mark.
- Micro-fine hairline borders and subtle tech grids instead of heavy drop shadows.
- Dedicated high-visibility call-to-action touchpoints for instant WhatsApp quoting and technical booking.

## Colors

The color palette directly extends the dynamic trinity node logo mark, maintaining pristine contrast and institutional trust.

- **Primary (`#082B4C` - Deep Navy Blue):** Establishes solid footing, technical mastery, and structural clarity. Used for primary headlines, dark-mode cards, top navigation backgrounds, and primary interface chrome.
- **Secondary (`#00A3E0` - Tech Cyan Blue):** Infuses digital energy and agility. Serves as active state highlights, interactive toggles, service indicators, and link highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` - Direct WhatsApp / Action Green):** Dedicated action color strictly reserved for WhatsApp contact triggers, quick-booking conversions, and "Available / Online" technician status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Cool Tech Slate):** Balances typography hierarchy, subtle border demarcations, and secondary metadata without visual fatigue.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC`):** Maintains a cleanroom, sterile tech environment that visually echoes meticulous hardware cleaning and dust-free assembly.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-tech engineering with ergonomic legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a crisp, technical aesthetic that mirrors precision hardware components and data circuits. Used with tighter tracking for titles, diagnostic service metrics, and card headers.
- **Body & Data Text (`Inter`):** Guarantees frictionless reading for repair breakdown lists, technical descriptions, diagnostic reports, and pricing charts.
- **Labels & Badges (`Space Grotesk` Uppercase/Semi-bold):** Used for hardware tags (e.g., `TROCA DE PASTA TÉRMICA`, `UPGRADE SSD`, `BANCADA LIMPA`) to give components a calibrated, laboratory grade feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by strict vertical rhythm to reflect surgical cleanliness.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1280px. Gutters set to 1.5rem (`24px`) and canvas margins at 3rem (`48px`). Sections breathe with 4rem (`64px`) to 6rem (`96px`) vertical spacing to avoid cluttered technical data.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins. Two-column service feature matrices reflow comfortably.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) margins. Cards, diagnostic steps, and price columns stack vertically to maximize one-thumb touch access.

## Elevation & Depth

To express cleanliness and technical precision, this design system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** combined with **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines**:

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` provides a calm backdrop that sets off white cards.
- **Card Tier 1 (Surface Elevation):** Solid `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a whisper-light ambient drop: `0px 2px 8px rgba(8, 43, 76, 0.04)`.
- **Card Tier 2 (Interactive Hover & Focus):** The border transitions to `rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.4)` with a technical elevation glow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.12)`.
- **Hero & Command Surfaces:** Deep Navy (`#082B4C`) surfaces utilize faint sub-surface cyan glow highlights (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.15), transparent 70%)`) to mirror high-end gaming rigs and motherboard PCBs.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a controlled, professional **Soft (`1`)** approach:
- Default components (buttons, input fields, cards, tabs) utilize `0.25rem` (`4px`) to `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radiuses.
- This creates crisp, structural, and architectural contours resembling precision chassis panels, RAM heat spreaders, and motherboard slots rather than soft or toy-like interfaces.
- Floating badges and WhatsApp communication pills preserve a selective pill radius (`9999px`) solely to indicate instantaneous communication and human touch.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Schedule / Diagnostic):** Deep Navy background (`#082B4C`), crisp white text, `0.375rem` radius, bold uppercase font (`Space Grotesk`). Hover state shifts to `#0C3B66` with subtle cyan accent border.
- **WhatsApp Instant CTA:** WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) fill with white text and WhatsApp chat icon. Hover state introduces subtle scale (`1.02`) and elevated green shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#082B4C` or `#00A3E0`. Text inherits border color.

### Service & Diagnosis Cards
- Crisp white surfaces with hairline slate borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- Header icon box features a soft cyan tint background (`rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.1)`) with primary cyan SVG icons (cooling fans, CPU dies, clean screwdrivers, thermal paste syringes).
- Distinct pricing or checklist breakdown with custom cyan checkmarks.

### Technical Badges & Chips
- Compact `label-sm` font in uppercase tracking.
- Styled as semi-transparent pills: e.g., "PREVENTIVA", "TROCA DE TELA", "UPGRADE SSD".
- Background: `rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.12)`, Text: `#007CA8`, Border: `1px solid rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.25)`.

### Input Fields & Quotation Forms
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with `1px` neutral border (`#CBD5E1`).
- Focus State: Smooth outline ring in `#00A3E0` with zero offset and `2px` width.
- Clear technical placeholders, e.g., "Ex: Notebook Dell Inspiron superaquecendo em jogos".

### Lists & Specification Rows
- Alternating subtle zebra striping (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with bottom divider lines in `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)`.
- Left-aligned item name in bold Slate Navy, right-aligned metric or status (e.g., "Tempo médio: 24h", "Garantia: 90 dias").

### Floating Quick-Contact Dock
- Anchored to the bottom right on mobile and desktop.
- Houses a direct click-to-chat button with an online indicator pulse animation to maximize conversion.
